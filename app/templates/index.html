    Limits you should know about

    • This is a research prototype. It is not validated for clinical use and is not a medical device.
    • A result describes one image patch, not a whole slide and not a patient.
    • It learned from public data. How it behaves on other scanners, stains or hospitals is unknown.
    • The image datasets and TCGA-STAD are different groups of people. Unless slides are matched to TCGA barcodes, fusion is a control experiment and can't show a multimodal benefit.
    • Grad-CAM and SHAP describe what the model did. Pathologists haven't validated them.
    Research prototype. Not clinically validated. Not for diagnosis or treatment decisions. A pathologist must review every result.
