    About & limits

    How the pipeline works, and what it cannot tell you.

    How a result is made

    1. The patch is resized and normalised, then classified by a CNN fine-tuned on histology tiles.
    2. Probabilities are calibrated with temperature scaling fitted on validation data only.
    3. Grad-CAM shows where the network looked; it explains the model, not the tissue.
    4. A distance check against the training features and simple stain and blur checks flag inputs the model was not built for.
    5. If you add age, sex, stage or gene expression, a separate TCGA-trained model estimates a molecular subtype with SHAP explanations.

    What it cannot do

    • It is not a medical device and does not diagnose.
    • Results describe a patch, or an aggregate of patches in slide mode, never a patient.
    • Performance on other scanners, stains or hospitals is unknown.
    • Image and genomic data come from different cohorts, so fusion is an experiment, not evidence of multimodal benefit.
    • Grad-CAM and SHAP have not been validated by pathologists.
