How an analysis flows

  1. UploadOne image patch. Age, sex, stage and a gene-expression file are optional.
  2. ClassifyImage features and class probabilities, calibrated on a validation split.
  3. ExplainGrad-CAM for the image; SHAP for clinical and genomic features.
  4. ReportEvery modality used or skipped is stated. Download the PDF.

    Limitations

    • Research prototype; not validated for clinical use and not a medical device.
    • Results describe one image patch, not a whole slide or a patient.
    • Public training data; performance on other scanners, stains or hospitals is unknown.
    • Image datasets and TCGA-STAD come from different cohorts. Unless slides are matched to TCGA barcodes, fusion is a control experiment and cannot show multimodal benefit.
    • Grad-CAM and SHAP describe model behaviour; they have not been validated by pathologists.
    Research prototype. Not clinically validated. Not for diagnosis or treatment decisions. A pathologist must review every result.
